EasyHotel expanded demo dataset
- guests.csv: 150 fictional guests
- rooms.csv: 60 rooms
- bookings.csv: 320 synthetic bookings
- payments.csv: demo payment records for non-cancelled bookings
- services.csv: 10 services
- reviews.csv: 100 reviews
- ai_knowledge.csv: 15 FAQ entries for a future chatbot
- hotel_info.csv: 10 settings

All data is fictional for educational use. Prices are sample EGP amounts.
No real payments are processed. CSV files are not a live database yet.
Synthetic bookings may overlap for the same room; the backend must check
room availability before confirming real bookings.
